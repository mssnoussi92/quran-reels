The service

Quran Words Daily is a personal, non-commercial project that publishes one short video of a Quran passage every day. The application Quran Words Daily Publisher is an automated tool used only by the project's operator to publish these videos to the project's own accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Content and sources

The Arabic text, the English translation (Sahih International), the word meanings (Ibn Qutaybah, Tafsir Gharib al-Qur'an) and the recitation (Mishary Rashid Alafasy) come from published sources and are credited in every video. They are not generated or rewritten by artificial intelligence. Background footage comes from free stock libraries.

If you hold rights to any material used and object to its use, contact us and we will remove it.

Platform rules

Publishing follows the terms and community guidelines of Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, including their developer terms.

No warranty

The videos are shared for learning and reflection. They are provided as is; for religious rulings or detailed interpretation, consult qualified scholars and complete works of tafsir.
